Leftover food from the canteen, in an NGO's hands before it spoils.

Canteen staff record surplus raw and prepared food. The engine ranks it by how soon it spoils, NGOs request it, and every hand-over is logged. A moving-average model forecasts tomorrow's surplus so the kitchen can cook less.
